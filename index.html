HAPPY BIRTHDAY, MY LOVE. ❤️

First of all, I’m so happy that you exist. That, out of billions of people in this world, I somehow got to meet you, know you and have you become such a beautiful part of my life. Sometimes I think about how randomly everything started between us, how neither of us probably imagined we’d end up here, and it makes me smile.

Today, I hope you take a moment to appreciate yourself, not just for the person you are today, but for everything you’ve overcome, everything you’ve learned and every little step that has brought you here. You’re getting another year older, but I hope you never lose the little things that make you who you are.

If I could give you anything today, I think I’d give you a little piece of every gentle thing this world has to offer: the warmth of sunlight on quiet mornings, the peace that comes after a long day, the comfort of knowing you are loved, and the kind of happiness that asks nothing from you except that you simply exist.

I hope life is soft with you. I hope it gives you reasons to smile when you least expect them, people who hold your heart carefully, opportunities that bring you closer to your dreams, and days that feel worth remembering.

And if there is one thing I wish you could always remember, it’s this: you are deeply precious to me. More than these little words could ever explain. I love you from a place so sincere and quiet that sometimes I don’t even know how to put it into language.

To live in this world, we feel so small. To swim in water, it feels slow. But to be with you was an unexpected surprise. It was like stumbling upon a starlit path in the darkest woods at midnight.

Sometimes, the things you want the most don’t happen, and what you least expect does. I could compare you to a thousand different things — the stars, the ocean, butterflies, the moon — but it’s really difficult to find words that could truly describe how much I adore you.

Your presence is like a sonnet that finds beauty in every syllable, every pause. And I will always be grateful that you made it this far.

I genuinely believe that you are an amazing person. I love you for all that you are, all that you have been, and all that you’re yet to become. Because for a long time, when I’ve felt empty or alone, it was you that I could call my home.

I want this new year of your life to be kinder to you. I hope you grow into someone you’re proud of. I hope you find things that make you passionate about life, achieve the things you’ve been dreaming about and never feel like you’re running out of time. I want to see you happy, healthy, successful and genuinely at peace with yourself.

I hope you never become too occupied with growing up that you forget to enjoy being alive. Keep laughing at silly things, keep discovering, keep being curious, keep making mistakes and learning from them. There’s so much life waiting for you, and I hope you get to experience all the beautiful parts of it.

And selfishly, I hope I get to witness some of it too. I hope I get to celebrate your little victories, listen to your endless stories, annoy you on your bad days, laugh with you over absolutely nothing and experience all those ordinary little moments that eventually become our favourite memories.

I don’t know exactly what every chapter of our story will look like, but I know that I’m grateful I got to meet you and experience this kind of love with you.

So, on your birthday, I just hope you stay. Keep growing, keep laughing, keep becoming whoever you are meant to be. And may life be gentle enough to let you do all of it peacefully.

Today, as we celebrate another year of your life, I want you to know that my love for you grows stronger and more profound with each passing day. I promise to keep choosing you, appreciating you and loving you through all the different versions of yourself.

Happy Birthday, my dear.

I hope the world takes good care of you, because you mean far more to me than you probably know.

I love you, my beanie baby. ❤️

your Heena. ♡
